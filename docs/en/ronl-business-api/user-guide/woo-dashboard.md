# Woo-dashboard

*woo · compliance*

Woo-dashboard is for steering on the Wet open overheid (Woo). It shows compliance, lead times, process bottlenecks and active publication, with traffic lights and a "Woo in cijfers" benchmark.

On opening the board you see traffic-light indicators for compliance and lead times, alongside the "Woo in cijfers" benchmark, so bottlenecks in the publication process stand out.

  ![Screenshot: RONL Business API Woo-dashboard showing compliance traffic lights and the Woo in cijfers benchmark](../../assets/screenshots/ronl-business-api-woo-dashboard-board.png)

**Opening it.** Sign in with your Flevoland account — the card's **Flevoland-account** button, or the
landing page's primary button — and the board opens if your account holds the Entra ID app role
`IOU_WOO`. Without it, the landing page's dialog — or the board itself, if you reach it another way —
says **Geen toegang** and names that role, so you know what to ask your functioneel beheerder for.
