# Woo-dashboard

*woo · compliance*

Woo-dashboard is for steering on the Wet open overheid (Woo). It shows compliance, lead times, process bottlenecks and active publication, with traffic lights and a "Woo in cijfers" benchmark.

On opening the board you see traffic-light indicators for compliance and lead times, alongside the "Woo in cijfers" benchmark, so bottlenecks in the publication process stand out.
