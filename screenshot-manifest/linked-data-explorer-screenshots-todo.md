**The replaced image was four releases stale, not one.** It dated from 30 March 2026
and showed **seven** icons where the application now has eleven plus Settings —
missing Form Editor, Document Composer, RoPA Records, DSO Explorer and the SHACL
Validator, every one of which has had its own documentation page since. It was only
asked for now because v2026.09.2 made the drift *assertable*: the release removed

reader could check against the running application in a second.

That is worth recording as a manifest lesson rather than a one-off. A screenshot
goes stale silently: nothing in a build fails, no link breaks, and the page around
it keeps being revised accurately. What caught this was a **removal** — an element
that used to be there and now is not — because removals are the only UI change a
reader can falsify from an old image without knowing what the new one should look
like. Additions just look like a cropped screenshot.

**The capture came in better framed than the row asked for.** The rail carries all
eleven icons with **Settings pinned at the bottom** — where it was being pushed off
the edge before v2026.09.2 — and the DMN list beside it shows its own scrollbar, so
the scrolling behaviour is legible rather than inferred.

**Why this one is worth re-shooting.** The current image predates two changes to
the same view. The modal was restructured because it had grown past the viewport

every miss silently and still showed a green *Resources to deploy* panel.

The second is the one a reader needs to see. Its absence produced a failure three
components away: `RipR21Process` deployed with its BPMN and twelve forms but none
of its three documents, and the RONL Business API's phase-exit approval then
rendered an ordinary form instead of the signing panel.
